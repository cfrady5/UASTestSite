Advancing real-world testing, validation and deployment

The Midwest UAS Test Site of Indiana provides a real-world environment where unmanned systems are tested in active airspace with the speed and access needed to move from concept to capability.

Built on Indiana's strengths in manufacturing, defense and research, the test site connects design, testing and deployment — supporting both commercial and national security applications.

Its purpose extends beyond validating functionality; it ensures that technologies are not only effective but also capable of scaling into operational, mission-ready capabilities.

  • End-to-End Ecosystem

  • Expanding Urban & BVLOS Testing

  • Defense Installation Access

Advancing the future of flight

Indiana's Midwest UAS Test Site is helping grow a domestic drone ecosystem by enabling real-world testing of advanced technologies. By supporting validation, manufacturing and deployment in one environment, Indiana is accelerating innovation while strengthening U.S.-based supply chains.

Test site capabilities

  • Defense & Counter-UAS

    Kinetic testing, electronic warfare (EMS) and threat mitigation.

  • Public Safety & Urban Operations

    Testing in complex environments, including GPS-denied areas, dense urban conditions and extended BVLOS corridors.

  • Commercial & Agriculture

    Logistics, precision agriculture and large-scale data collection.

Ecosystem & network partners

A statewide network of academic, defense and research infrastructure — connected by multi-domain airspace and major logistics corridors.
