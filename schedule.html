Range access follows a five-step process, from creating a member account through to reservation approval.

  1. 01

    Create a Member Account

  2. 02

    Request a Range Reservation

  3. 03

    Receive / Accept Coordination Invite

  4. 04

    Complete Required Documentation

  5. 05

    Receive Reservation Approval

Creating a member account begins the process. It does not approve range access or create a reservation — scheduling is confirmed by the test site during coordination.
